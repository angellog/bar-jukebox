# QueuePlay: Kampala prospect list (Oct 2026)

Each venue was checked against its own website, the KCCA Visit Kampala portal, Pulse Uganda or Nile Post coverage, or its live Instagram profile. Emails appear only where the venue publishes them on its own site or KCCA listing; none were guessed. Follower counts were checked in Oct 2026.

**Warnings**
- **Thrones and Cielo:** their old website domains now show gambling spam. Use Instagram DM.
- **Skyz Hotel:** no longer part of Marriott (its site says "Formerly Protea by Marriott"). Pitch it on its own.
