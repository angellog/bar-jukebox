Welcome to QueuePlay 🎶

Four steps and your guests can start picking songs. About 10 minutes.

  1. Connect Spotify. Link the venue's Spotify Premium account in Music & Speaker.
  2. Choose your speaker. A phone or tablet with Spotify on your sound system (no screen needed), a smart speaker, or a laptop with the Player page.
  3. Turn on background music so it never goes quiet. Guest requests always play next.
  4. Print your QR code and put it on tables and the bar. Then test with your phone.

The checklist on your Overview tab tracks your progress. Your links are in Settings.
