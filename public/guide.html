Venue setup guide

From sign-up to your first guest request. Follow the steps in order; each one says exactly where to click in your dashboard.

⏱ About 10 minutes
0

Before you start

  • A Spotify Premium account for the venue. Use one account just for the venue. If staff play music on it elsewhere (say, on their personal phone), Spotify moves the venue's music there.
  • A device connected to your speakers: an old phone or tablet with the Spotify app, a Spotify Connect speaker (Sonos, Echo, Google Nest), a smart TV or Chromecast, or a laptop.
  • Wi-Fi for that device. Guests can use Wi-Fi or mobile data.
Early access: before you connect Spotify, your venue's Spotify account must be activated by the QueuePlay team. Enter its email when you sign up, or send it to the QueuePlay team. Activation is usually same-day.
1

Create your venue

  1. Go to Create a venue.
  2. Enter the venue name, choose the type (it sets suggested background genres), and add the Spotify account email.
  3. Choose an admin password. Staff use it to log in to the dashboard and the player.
  4. Click Set up my venue. You land in your dashboard with a setup checklist on the Overview tab.
Your links are always under Settings → Your Venue Links. To get back to the dashboard later, use Log in and type your venue link.
2

Connect Spotify

  1. In the dashboard, open Music & Speaker.
  2. Under 1 · Spotify account, click Connect Spotify Account.
  3. Log in with the venue's Premium account and click Agree. You return to the dashboard with ✅ Spotify connected.
Dashboard → Music & Speaker → 1 · Spotify account
3

Choose where music plays

Pick one. You can switch any time.

📱 Spotify speaker or app (no screen)

  1. Plug a phone or tablet into your sound system (or use a Spotify Connect speaker / smart TV).
  2. Open Spotify on it, logged in to the venue account, and play any song for a second so Spotify sees it.
  3. In 2 · Where music plays, choose Spotify speaker or app, press ↻ Refresh, select the device, then Save speaker.

💻 This browser (Player page)

  1. On a laptop or tablet connected to the speakers, open the Player (sidebar link "Open Player").
  2. Enter the admin password if asked, then tap Start Player.
  3. Keep that tab open. It shows Now Playing and Up next, which is handy on a screen behind the bar.
Phone as speaker? Turn on Do Not Disturb and keep it charging so notifications and battery saver don't interrupt the music.
4

Background music for quiet moments

When nobody has requested a song, QueuePlay keeps playing. Guest requests always play next, as soon as the current track ends.

  1. In 3 · Background music, tick Play background music when the request queue is empty.
  2. Choose Genre mix and pick up to 8 genres (suggestions are pre-selected for your venue type), or choose A Spotify playlist and pick one of yours or paste any playlist link.
  3. Click Save background music.
Want a polished sound? A playlist you've curated sounds more consistent than a genre mix.
5

Set guest rules

Open Settings → Guest Rate Limits. Each guest's phone is limited separately.

  • Cooldown between songs: minutes a guest waits before their next request. 0 = no cooldown.
  • Songs per guest per day.
  • Max songs in queue: new requests are refused when it's full.

Presets: Party (no limits), Balanced (5 min, 10/day, 25 in queue), Strict (15 min, 3/day, 15 in queue). Under Playback you can also block explicit songs.

Testing with your own phone? Use ↺ Reset all guests' limits now so the cooldown doesn't slow you down.
6

Make it yours

In Brand Identity, add your logo link, colours, font and a welcome message. Guests see them on your page, and the QR code uses your main colour.

7

Print and place your QR code

  1. Open Branded QR Code and click 💾 Download Image (or 📋 Copy Link to share the guest link on WhatsApp or Instagram).
  2. Print it with a short line like "Pick the next song: scan me".
  3. Place it where people sit and wait: table tents, the bar counter, menus, the entrance.
Print at least 4 cm wide so phones can scan it from a seat. Test one print before printing all of them.
8

Test run (2 minutes)

  • Music & Speaker → Start music. Background music plays (if switched on).
  • Scan the QR code with your phone, search a song, tap Add.
  • Add a second song from another phone.
  • Your songs play in order after the current track, without the current one being cut off.
  • Try Skip and Pause / Resume from the dashboard.

All good? You're live. 🎉

✓

Every day

  • Opening: turn on the speaker device, open Spotify on it (or the Player page), then Start music in the dashboard.
  • During service: remove unwanted requests in Queue & Controls; skip or pause from Music & Speaker.
  • Closing: press Stop, and Clear Queue (Queue & Controls) if you want a fresh start tomorrow.

Troubleshooting

"Spotify not connected" or guests see "This venue hasn't connected Spotify yet"

Go to Music & Speaker → Connect Spotify Account and log in with the venue's Premium account. If Spotify shows an error, the account may not be activated yet. See "Early access" in step 0.

"Speaker not found" / my device isn't in the list

Open Spotify on the device, make sure it's logged in to the venue account, play any song for a second, then press ↻ Refresh. Spotify only lists devices that are switched on with Spotify open.

Music suddenly moved to someone's phone

Someone played music with the venue's Spotify account on another device. Spotify plays on one device per account. Keep the venue account for the venue only, then press Start music again.

"The player was opened in another tab or device"

Only one Player page can run per venue. The newest one takes over. Close the extra tabs and tap Start Player on the one connected to the speakers.

A guest says "Please wait X minutes"

That's the cooldown from Settings → Guest Rate Limits. Lower it, set it to 0, or press Reset all guests' limits.

"Spotify refused playback"

The account needs Spotify Premium, and some devices (like certain smart TVs or cars) don't allow remote control. Try a phone with the Spotify app or the Player page.

I forgot my dashboard link

Use Log in and type your venue name. Your link is the venue name in lowercase with dashes, e.g. "Cloud Nine Lounge" → cloud-nine-lounge.

Still stuck? Contact the QueuePlay team.
