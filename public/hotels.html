The guest journey

Discover

A QR code on the room TV welcome screen, an in-room card, the key-card sleeve, and table cards in each space.

Choose a space

The hotel page lists open zones, e.g. Pool Bar · open until 22:00, Rooftop · from 17:00. Closed zones show their hours.

Request

Search, tap Add, and optionally enter a room number so staff can say "this one's for room 214". No app, no account.

Enjoy

The guest sees their place in the queue. The song plays in that space when its turn comes.

Music inside the rooms stays private. QueuePlay plays in shared spaces. It doesn't push music into guest rooms. Guests who want their own music in the room can use their TV's casting feature as usual.

Notes for the IT team

  • Nothing to install on your servers. QueuePlay is cloud-hosted; guest pages and the dashboard run in the browser over HTTPS.
  • Works with isolated guest networks. Phones and players never talk to each other directly. Everything goes through the cloud, so client isolation and VLANs are fine.
  • Outbound HTTPS only is needed from player devices (to QueuePlay and the music service).
  • Room TVs: hospitality TV platforms (e.g. LG Pro:Centric, Samsung LYNK) often restrict browsing, so a QR code on the welcome screen is the most reliable entry point. Where the TV browser is open, the TV-friendly page works with the remote.
  • Guest data: no account or login. We store the song, the zone and, only if the guest enters it, a room number, kept for 30 days for statistics. No names, phone numbers or payment details.

Music licensing

Playing music in a hotel's public spaces requires:

  1. A business music service. Consumer streaming accounts (personal Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube) are licensed for personal use only and can't be used for public playback. We'll agree a business-licensed music source with you as part of the proposal.
  2. A public-performance licence from the Uganda Performing Right Society (UPRS) for each property. Many hotels already hold one.

We'll confirm both before going live.

Questions

Can guests request songs from their room for the pool or rooftop?

Yes. That's the idea: they choose the zone, and the song plays there when its turn comes. Zones that are closed don't accept requests.

Can a guest flood the queue?

No. Limits are per room or phone: a cooldown between requests, a daily maximum and a maximum queue length per zone, plus optional staff approval.

What if the internet drops?

The current song finishes and background music resumes when the connection returns. Staff are shown the zone's status in the dashboard.

Do we need new speakers?

Usually not. A tablet or phone connects to your existing sound system in each zone.

Can it work across several of our properties?

Yes. Each property has its own zones under one group account.
