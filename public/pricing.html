Frequently asked questions

Do I need Spotify Premium?
Yes, the venue owner needs a Spotify Premium account to play full songs through the speakers. Guests do not need any Spotify account -- they just search and add.
Can guests install an app?
No app needed. Guests scan a QR code and land on a mobile-optimized web page. It works instantly in any browser.
How do I control what gets played?
From your dashboard you can start, pause and skip, remove any song from the queue, block explicit content, and set limits per guest (cooldown and songs per day).
Can I use my own branding?
Yes, on every plan: your logo, colours, font and welcome message on the guest page, plus a QR code in your colours.
How do I pay?
From your dashboard (Plan & Billing), pay for 1, 3 or 12 months with MTN or Airtel Mobile Money or a card, securely through Flutterwave. 12 months costs the price of 10. Nothing renews automatically; we remind you before your plan ends.
What happens when the queue is empty?
Turn on background music and QueuePlay keeps playing from genres or a playlist you choose. Guest requests always play next.
