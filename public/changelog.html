Changelog

What's new in QueuePlay.

1.4 · Plans & payments

12 October 2026New
  • Plan & Billing in the dashboard: pay for 1, 3 or 12 months (12 for the price of 10) with MTN or Airtel Mobile Money or card, through Flutterwave.
  • Prices now in Ugandan shillings. Plans are prepaid and never renew automatically; you get a reminder before yours ends.
  • Plan descriptions now list exactly what each plan changes.
  • Behind the scenes: one-click platform backups and a restore tool.

1.3 · Security & privacy

12 October 2026New
  • Change your admin password in Settings. Changing it signs out every other device.
  • Repeated wrong passwords are now temporarily blocked.
  • Forgot your password? The QueuePlay team can issue a temporary one.
  • New Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Guest IP addresses are now erased after 30 days.

1.2 · Onboarding

7 October 2026New
  • Setup guide: step-by-step from sign-up to your first guest request, with troubleshooting.
  • Welcome walkthrough for new venues and a setup checklist on the dashboard that ticks itself off as you go.
  • Venue log in: type your venue name to reach your dashboard.
  • Sign-up asks for the venue's Spotify account so it can be activated faster.
  • Refreshed website, plus a proposal for hotels.

1.1.1

2 October 2026Fix
  • Fixed a rare case where the next song could be queued twice.

1.1 · Speaker mode & background music

2 October 2026New
  • No screen needed: play through a phone or tablet with Spotify, a Spotify Connect speaker or a smart TV, controlled from the dashboard.
  • Background music when nobody is requesting: a genre mix suited to your venue, or a Spotify playlist. Guest requests always play next.
  • Up next list on the Player page.
  • Smoother transitions: the next song is lined up before the current one ends.

1.0 · First full release

2 October 2026New
  • Requests play in the order they're added, and adding a song never interrupts the current one.
  • Editable guest rate limits with presets, including "no cooldown".
  • Your venue links always available in Settings.
  • Simple password log in for staff (no keys to copy).
  • One player per venue, so music no longer stops and starts when two players are open.

Early access

June 2026
  • Guest QR requests, white-label branding, Spotify connection, admin dashboard.
