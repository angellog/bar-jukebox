Questions

How many guests can request songs?

As many as are in the room. Each phone is limited by the rules you set: a cooldown between requests and a daily maximum. The queue has a maximum length you choose.

What happens when nobody is requesting?

Turn on background music and QueuePlay keeps playing from the genres or playlist you picked. When a guest requests a song, it plays as soon as the current track ends.

Do I need a screen or a laptop?

No. Any phone or tablet with the Spotify app plugged into your sound system works, as does a Spotify Connect speaker or smart TV. You control everything from the dashboard on your own phone.

Will a new request cut off the song that's playing?

Never. New requests join the end of the queue and play in order.

Do guests need an app or a Spotify account?

No. They scan the QR code and use your page in their phone's browser. Only the venue needs Spotify Premium.

Can I stop explicit songs?

Yes. Switch on "Block explicit content" in Settings and explicit tracks won't appear in guest searches or background music. You can also remove any song from the queue.
